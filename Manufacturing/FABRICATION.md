Everything here is generated by `Source/export_manufacturing.py` from `PCB/` and `Plate/`.

**Before paying, check the placement preview for every bottom part**: socket orientation (its two
metal tabs on the two large pads), the U1 pin-1 dot, the U4 pin-1 dot, the D1-D82 cathode bars on
the ROW pad, and the J1 opening at the board edge. Bottom-side rotations are the most common CPL
error. Test pads TP1-TP4 and fiducials FID1-FID3 are deliberately left out of the BOM and CPL.

## 2. FR4 plate (order it as a PCB)

Files in `Plate/`: `Vamora75_Plate_gerbers.zip` (standard) and `Vamora75_Plate_flexcut_gerbers.zip`
(relief slots behind each gasket tab). The KiCad sources are in `../Plate/`.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Layers | 2 (copper is used only for the "Vamora75" name) |
| Thickness | **1.5 mm** if offered, otherwise 1.6 mm (fits; gaskets compress about 0.05 mm more) |
| Surface finish | **ENIG** gives a gold name; HASL gives silver |
| Mask / silkscreen | Black recommended / white |
| Notes | 98 routed openings, no drilled holes, minimum web 1.56 mm, slots 1.2 mm |

The plate is also provided as DXF and STEP (`../Mechanical/Plate/`) for laser-cut aluminium, brass,
POM or PC. Use the same outline. For metal plates, check that the switch clips fit the thickness.

## 3. Case

`../Mechanical/Case/`: one-piece STEP/STL for CNC or for printers with a bed of 360 mm or more.
`../Mechanical/Case/print_split/`: four pieces (the largest is about 224 x 161 mm) for 250 x 210 mm beds.
The seams are staggered, and each one has dowel holes (3.1 mm, 6 mm deep per side). The bottom seam is placed in a gap between two letters of the engraved name.

* **3D printing (PETG or ASA):** print the bottom on its flat underside (the engraved "Vamora75" prints
  as a 0.6 mm recess in the first layers), and print the top upside down (keyboard face on the bed).
  Use 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 20 % gyroid infill and 5 top/bottom layers. The insert holes are
  4.0 mm for M3 x 4 heat-set inserts.
* **CNC (6061-T6):** use the one-piece STEP. Tap M3 instead of using inserts. The solid wedge under
  the floor can be pocketed from below to save weight; keep 2.5 mm under the floor and leave the
  screw bosses solid.
