Everything here is generated by `Source/export_manufacturing.py` from `PCB/` and `Plate/`.
Regenerate it after any design change.

**Rev 1.6 USB routing:** USB-C stays 25.00 mm from the PCB's left edge. The RP2040
is now at board-local (40, 23) mm, with its flash and crystal nearby. Keep R3/R4 at
**27 Ω**: the RP2040 datasheet requires external series resistors despite the integrated PHY.

The uniform B.Cu pair uses **0.2276 mm width, 0.1501 mm pair gap and 0.1999 mm coplanar
ground gap**, referenced to **In2.Cu (L3)**. F.Cu and In1.Cu carry other signals;
In2.Cu is reserved for ground. The JLCPCB calculator was checked with solder mask,
outer 1 oz / inner 0.5 oz copper and JLC04161H-7628 on 2026-11-11.
See `../Reference/JLCPCB_USB_impedance_2026-11-11.json` for the inputs and result.
The ESD input nets `USB_CONN_D+/-` end at U2 pins 3/1; outputs on pins 4/6
continue as `USB_D+/-`. The channels connect inside the USBLC6, with no PCB bypass.
C8 is 100 nF, 0402, LCSC C1525 / CL05B104KO5NNNC, on VBUS near U2.
Short pad escapes and transitions are impedance discontinuities; the calculator does
not certify the assembled USB channel. Request stackup/geometry review and the **90 Ω ±10%
coupon report**. Do not accept an unreviewed stackup substitution or automatic trace change.

Copper-to-copper stack (top to bottom, mm): 0.035 / 0.2104 prepreg / 0.0152 /
1.065 core / 0.0152 / 0.2104 prepreg / 0.035. Prepreg Er 4.4, core Er 4.6.
The build checks actual filled ground beneath the uniform pair; DRC is not a USB
signal-quality or compliance test. Verify both cable orientations, enumeration and
repeated reconnects on the first assembled board.
[JLCPCB impedance calculator](https://jlcpcb.com/pcb-impedance-calculator/).

**Select black solder mask on the order.** Gerbers do not encode the purchased mask colour.

needs Standard), tooling holes added by JLCPCB.

**Before paying, check the placement preview for every bottom part**: socket orientation (its two
metal tabs on the two large pads), the U1 pin-1 dot, the U4 pin-1 dot, the D1-D82 cathode bars on
the ROW pad, and the J1 opening at the board edge. Bottom-side rotations are the most common CPL
error. Test pads TP1-TP4 and fiducials FID1-FID3 are deliberately left out of the BOM and CPL.

## 2. FR4 plate (order it as a PCB)

Files in `Plate/`: `Vamora75_Plate_gerbers.zip` (standard) and `Vamora75_Plate_flexcut_gerbers.zip`
(relief slots behind each gasket tab). The KiCad sources are in `../Plate/`.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Layers | 2 (copper is used only for the "Vamora75" name) |
| Thickness | **1.5 mm** if offered, otherwise 1.6 mm (fits; gaskets compress about 0.05 mm more) |
| Surface finish | **ENIG** gives a gold name; HASL gives silver |
| Mask / silkscreen | Black recommended / white |
| Notes | 90 routed openings (98 for the flex-cut version), no drilled holes, stabiliser openings 7.0 x 14 mm, minimum web 1.44 mm, slots 1.2 mm |

The plate is also provided as DXF and STEP (`../Mechanical/Plate/`) for laser-cut aluminium, brass,
POM or PC. Use the same outline. For metal plates, check that the switch clips fit the thickness.

## 3. Case

`../Mechanical/Case/`: CNC D5 one-piece STEP/STL, with blind M3 pilot holes.
`../Mechanical/Case/print_one_piece/`: separate one-piece print variants with heat-set insert bores.
`../Mechanical/Case/print_split/`: four pieces (the largest is about 215 x 157 mm) for 250 x 210 mm beds.
The seams are staggered, and each one has dowel holes (3.1 mm, 6 mm deep per side). The bottom seam is placed in a gap between two letters of the engraved name.

* **PC-CF on CORE One L:** use [Vamora75_PCCF_print.zip](Vamora75_PCCF_print.zip),
  with print-oriented halves and an insert coupon. Follow [PCCF_PRINT.md](PCCF_PRINT.md).
  Budget three 800 g Prusament PC-CF spools for three cases; confirm with the slicer.
* **Alternative PETG or ASA:** print the bottom on its flat underside (the engraved "Vamora75" prints
  as a 0.6 mm recess in the first layers), and print the top upside down (keyboard face on the bed;
  the bridges between key clusters then print flat on the bed, no supports).
  Use 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 20 % gyroid infill and 5 top/bottom layers. The insert holes are
  4.0 mm for M3 x 4 heat-set inserts.
* **CNC D5 (6061-T6):** upload `Case_Drawings/Vamora75_case_CNC_D5_package.zip`.
  Finish is as machined and deburred, without coating. General dimensions +/-0.10 mm;
  critical tolerances, datums and inspection requirements are on sheet 3 and `CNC_REQUIREMENTS.txt`.
  Top: eight M3 x 0.5-6H blind threads, full form from 0.40 to at least 5.00 mm below the mating face.
  STEP contains 2.50 mm flat-bottom pilots, depth 5.70 +/-0.05 mm, and entry chamfers.
  Thread-mill these holes; do not leave them unthreaded or add a drill-point cone beyond depth 5.75.
  Bottom: 3.40 mm clearance, 6.50 mm counterbores, head bearing seats 3.50 +/-0.05 mm below mating face.
  Use eight M3 x 8 ISO 4762 screws without washers: nominal insertion 4.50 mm.
  Make one matched pair and verify the physical assembly before repeating the remaining two.
  Keep the floor and screw regions as modeled; do not add weight-saving pockets without a new revision.
